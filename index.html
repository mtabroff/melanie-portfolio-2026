selected work

work

  1. Somethings 2025-2026

    Somethings connects teens with mentors who've been through similar struggles, using that relationship as a mental health intervention.

    i loved the mission, and i joined to help shape the teen experience and solve for retention. the first real swing was re-architecting the app around two things: chat, and a shared "hub" — a space to reflect, share, and connect beyond conversation. the core problem was keeping teens coming back and engaged even when their mentor was offline.

  2. Somethings.com

    Somethings 2025-2026

    one of the early features we shipped was photo “swaps”, built on a simple insight: if a mentor opens up first, teens are more likely to follow.

    photo swaps made that easy: a mentor and teen each send a photo to the same prompt, and it reveals for both at once. it was especially effective for teens who were still warming up to their mentor.

    the harder problem was making it scale: mentors were each juggling 20+ teens, sometimes dealing with very high-risk problems, so whatever we asked of them had to be lightweight.

  3. Somethings.com

    Somethings 2025

    when i joined Somethings, we were in the process of redesigning our website with the agency Love&Money to add legitimacy to our brand as we expanded to new markets.
    what started as a website overhaul blossomed into a full brand identity evolution.

    i had a lot of fun giving feedback on art direction while translating the new brand to our product and marketing surfaces, building a mobile design system and marketing kit along the way.

  4. Titan 2025

    Titan is a mobile-first wealth management platform.

    over my three years there, we expanded into sophisticated territory, from alternative investments to DIY stock trading. this project was the swing back to simplicity: moving from "set it and forget it" DIY investing to a more human, advisor-led experience, right as Titan crossed $1b in AUM.

    the challenge was creating one clear feeling: i trust these people with my money. that meant making the experience simple enough to feel like you're in good hands, while preserving the detail savvy investors need to understand their money.

    myself and two other designers led the end-to-end redesign, a project that leaned hard on rapid prototyping, storytelling, and navigating real ambiguity.

  5. Titan 2024

    while Titan was mid-rebrand, we were also testing a new lifestyle vertical — Titan Concierge — offering clients exclusive drops, restaurant reservations, and access to the Titan booth at MSG, aimed at pulling in a younger demographic.

    it was a fast-moving, cross-functional build: my team worked in tandem with marketing and partnerships, shipping updates based on client feedback in near real time.

    the vertical was eventually sunset, but the pace of that collaboration — build, ship, learn, adjust — was some of the best cross-functional work i did at Titan.

  6. Titan 2025

    i had the pleasure of working with the agency Collins on Titan’s rebrand.

    as the most senior designer on the team, i took their brand guidelines and translated them into the product, building the design system alongside our lead engineer.

    i then extended the brand through two new visual systems: app-wide icons and illustrations for our investment strategies and money movement flows. i hired and directed an icon designer myself, and collaborated closely with a Collins-recommended illustrator to shape the work, which eventually became part of the system itself.

    directing that icon and illustration work pushed me outside my usual product-design lane, and gave me an appreciation for all the work that goes into creating a sophisticated brand.

  7. Titan 2023

    Smart Cash was Titan's cash product — something like a high-yield savings account, but more tax-efficient and higher-yielding thanks to treasury bonds, and built to let clients move money straight into investments.

    under the creative direction of our head of design, i designed the landing pages for funded and unfunded clients, as well as flows for referrals and money movements — each with its own small details and edge cases to work through.

    it taught me to zoom out and think about everything a new product touches on its way to market.

    Smart Cash went on to become one of Titan's most successful launches, driving over 30% of our AUM.

  8. Allarahealth.com

    Allara 2025

    Allara is a telehealth platform built for women navigating hard-to-treat hormonal and metabolic conditions.

    Allara approached me to help build a design system for their client-facing portal. they had no in-house designer at the time (just a PM), and prototyping new ideas was slow going without one. a system meant both polish and speed, and it would ideally let non-designers contribute more easily too.

    i built the whole thing by hand, with ChatGPT only for naming conventions.

    if i started this project today, i'd lean on AI a lot more and probably get further, faster. but there's something to building it manually first: it's part of why i trust myself with systems now.
